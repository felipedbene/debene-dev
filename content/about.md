I'm **Felipe De Bene** — Cloud Architect at AWS by day, and the reason there's a
2001 Power Mac G5 in a Chicago basement controlling Spotify over the Gopher protocol
by night.

This blog is where the two halves meet: the infrastructure and enterprise UNIX I do
for a living, and the alternative-computing rabbit holes I fall into for fun. AIX on
POWER8. Kubernetes homelabs behind Cloudflare Tunnels. Vintage Macs coaxed into doing
modern things they were never designed for. I write it up because explaining the
non-sense is how I actually learn the real stuff.

## What I build

- **The Gopherverse** — modern services delivered over the 1991 Gopher protocol
  (RFC 1436): Spotify remotes for Mac OS 9, Tiger and Snow Leopard, a native Android
  Gopher client, a phlog renderer, and a shared Rust core.
- **Chicago 'L' trackers** — live CTA train positions rendered three ways: a
  NORAD-style board on Cloudflare Workers, a Rust/ratatui terminal app, and a
  Unicode-braille Gopher map.
- **Gandula** — a text-based Brazilian football manager, with a Rust/WASM engine, a
  reinforcement-learning agent, and a native Android port.

Whatever the machine needs: `Rust` · `Kotlin` · `Go` · `TypeScript` · `C` ·
`Objective-C` · `Python` · `Kubernetes` · `Cloudflare` · `AWS`.

**Fun fact:** I learned BGP the hard way — by taking 1,400+ branches offline with a
single command.
